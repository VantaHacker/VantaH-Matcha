# VantaH-Matcha

Matcha scripts. Made by VantaH.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## Dandy's World
ESP and automation for Dandy's World.
```lua
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VantaHacker/VantaH-Matcha/refs/heads/main/DW.lua"))()
```

### Visuals
- Monsters, Items, Tapes and Research Capsules, each with its own toggle and colour
- Ichor Extractors with live fill percent, minigame type and a separate colour when done
- Highlights extractors that are already full of other players
- Dot, tracer, distance, name, room and rarity, all toggleable
- Every colour is editable in the menu

### Alerts
- 15 twisteds and 10 items you can individually watch
- Notifies once per spawn and highlights the thing in pink
- Optional extra tracers for watched things only

### Automation
- Auto Skill Check for all three types: bar, circle and treadmill
- Auto Barnaby plays the SwimmyBarnaby minigame, in machines and at the lobby arcade
- Optional coin collecting, with a riskier profile if you want more of them
- Randomised press timing
- Only presses while Roblox is focused

Settings save automatically and come back on rejoin.
